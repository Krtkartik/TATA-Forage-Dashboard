Tata Forage Simulation -- Sales Analytics Dashboard

📊 Project Overview

This project is a Sales Analytics Dashboard created as part of the
Tata Forage Simulation.

The dashboard is designed to present sales performance in an interactive
and easy-to-understand format. It combines KPIs, sales trends,
country-level analysis, product performance, quantity analysis, and
stock-code level details into a single Power BI dashboard.

The main purpose of the dashboard is to convert raw sales data into
useful business insights through visual analysis.

🎯 Project Objectives

The dashboard focuses on the following objectives:
Monitor overall sales performance.
Track sales trends over time.
Compare sales across countries.
Identify high-performing products.
Analyse sales distribution by country.
Understand the relationship between sales and quantity.
Analyse stock-code level sales and quantity.
Provide a compact business view using KPIs and interactive visuals.

🛠️ Tool Used

Power BI
Power BI was used to:
Import and analyse the sales data.
Create calculated values and KPIs.
Build interactive charts.
Apply filters and slicers.
Compare sales across different dimensions.
Present the final analysis in a dashboard format.

📈 Dashboard Components

1. Total Sales KPI
Visual: Card
The dashboard displays:
Sum of Total Sales: 9.90K
This KPI provides a quick view of the total sales represented by the
current dashboard/filter context.
It is useful as the primary high-level sales indicator.

2. Monthly Sales Trend

Visual: Line Chart
This visual shows the change in total sales over time.

Purpose

Understand sales movement across years.
Identify whether sales are increasing or decreasing.
Observe changes in sales performance over time.
The displayed chart shows an increase from the earlier period towards
2011.

3. Sales by Country

Visual: Bar Chart

This visual compares the Sum of Total Sales by Country.
The dashboard currently shows:
Austria as the displayed country.
Sales are approximately 9.9K in the shown context.
Business Use
Country-level analysis can help identify:
Markets generating higher sales.
Geographic differences in performance.
Countries that may require additional business attention.

4. Total Sales by Description and Country

Visual: Horizontal Bar Chart
This chart analyses sales according to product description and
country.
The visual contains product descriptions such as:

POSTAGE
PACK OF 6 PAN...
PACK OF 6 SWE...
EDWARDIAN PA...
RETROSPOT TE...
DOLLY GIRL BA...
BREAD BIN DIN...

Purpose

This visual helps identify which product descriptions contribute more to
sales within the selected country/filter context.

5. StockCode and Total Revenue by Country

Visual: Column Chart
This visual combines:
Count of StockCode
Sum of Total Revenue
Country
The current displayed country is Austria.

Purpose

It provides a combined view of:
Number of stock-code records.
Revenue generated.
Country-level performance.
This can help compare product/stock-code activity with revenue.

6. Sales Distribution by Country

Visual: Pie/Donut Chart
This chart shows the distribution of total sales across countries.
In the displayed dashboard state:
Austria represents the visible sales distribution.
Total displayed sales are approximately 9.9K.
The chart shows approximately 97.47% for the displayed segment.

Purpose
This visual provides a quick understanding of how sales are distributed
across the selected country context.

7. Business Insight Cards

The dashboard contains a dedicated insight section showing:
Highest Revenue
UK
Top-Selling Product
Black Mug
Sales Peak
Nov--Dec 2023

These cards provide quick business takeaways without requiring the user
to interpret every chart individually.
These are the values displayed in the dashboard screenshot and should
be interpreted according to the dashboard's underlying filter context.

8. Quantity KPI

Visual: KPI
The dashboard displays:
683.20
with a goal of:
148
and a displayed variance of:
+361.62%

Purpose

This KPI compares the current quantity value against the defined goal.
It gives a quick indication of how the displayed quantity compares with
the target.

9. Total Sales Gauge

Visual: Gauge
The gauge displays:
9.90K
against a displayed scale that reaches approximately 19.79K.

Purpose

The gauge provides a visual representation of the current total sales
value against the available range.

10. Monthly Sales Trend -- Area Chart

Visual: Area Chart
This visual compares:
Sum of Total Sales
Sum of Quantity
Invoice Date

Purpose

It provides another view of sales movement over time and allows sales
and quantity to be considered together.
This can help identify periods where changes in sales may correspond
with changes in quantity.

11. Sales by Year and Country

Visual: Column Chart
This chart compares the Sum of Total Sales by Year and Country.
The displayed chart contains:
Year
Country
Total Sales

Purpose

It helps analyse yearly sales performance while considering
country-level information.

12. Quantity and Sales by StockCode

Visual: Treemap
The treemap combines:
StockCode
Quantity
Total Sales
Each block represents a stock code, with the visual size representing
the selected measure.

Purpose

This visual is useful for identifying:
High-volume stock codes.
Stock codes contributing strongly to sales.
Differences between product volume and sales contribution.

It provides a more detailed product-level view than the high-level KPI
cards.

📝 Conclusion

The Tata Forage Simulation Sales Analytics Dashboard provides a
compact and interactive view of sales performance.
It combines KPIs, time-based trends, country analysis, product analysis,
revenue indicators, quantity performance, and stock-code details into
one dashboard.

The project demonstrates how Power BI can be used to transform sales
data into a clear visual report that supports business analysis and
faster decision-making.
